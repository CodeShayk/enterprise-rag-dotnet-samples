heading: # RX flexible coupling: installation and alignment
paragraph: Align the shafts with a dial indicator or a laser alignment tool after the gearbox and the motor are bolted down. Permissible misalignment for RX couplings up to size 90:
table: | Misalignment | Limit | / |---|---| / | Parallel offset | 0.05 mm | / | Angular | 0.05 mm per 100 mm | / | Axial gap | 3 mm ± 0.5 mm |
paragraph: Re-check the alignment after the first 100 hours of operation, because the foundation settles. Replace the elastomer element when its teeth show cracks or are worn by more than 25 percent.
table: RX coupling sizes /  / | Size | Maximum bore | Maximum torque | Elastomer element | / |---|---|---|---| / | 50 | 50 mm | 450 N·m | PN-5120-A | / | 70 | 70 mm | 1,250 N·m | PN-5170-A | / | 90 | 90 mm | 2,800 N·m | PN-5190-A | / | 110 | 110 mm | 5,200 N·m | PN-5210-A |
paragraph: For couplings above size 90, halve the permissible misalignment in the table above.
