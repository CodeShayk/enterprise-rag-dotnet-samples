# Safety bulletin SB-0932: Seal housing work

Issued after a near miss at the Riverside plant in 2026, when a seal housing was opened on a pump that was still under seal-water pressure.

Apply lockout/tagout (LOTO) to the motor and isolate the seal-water and process lines before opening any CP-200 seal housing. Verify zero energy before work starts.

Zero energy means that the motor can't start from the panel, the local pressure gauges read zero and the casing drain is open. Each person working on the pump fits their own lock.
