# Field service report SR-2026-1107

Customer: Tailspin Water Utility, raw-water pumping station 3. Engineer: Kwame Asante. Date: November 7, 2026.

Complaint: the CP-200 on duty tripped four times in two days with error E-217.

Findings: the seal-water pressure dropped to 1.2 bar before each trip. The seal-water strainer was almost fully blocked with sand from the raw-water supply. The mechanical seal (PN-4471-B) showed early face wear.

Work done: cleaned the strainer, replaced the seal with PN-4471-C under ECN-2291, and recommended a duplex strainer so the screen can be cleaned without stopping the pump. No further trips in 72 hours of monitoring.
