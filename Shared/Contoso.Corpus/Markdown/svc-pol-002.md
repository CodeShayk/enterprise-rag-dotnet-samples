# SVC-POL-002: Response times for contracted customers

Field Services commits to these response times for customers with a service contract, measured from the time the call is logged:

| Priority | Definition | On-site response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Production stopped, or a safety risk | 4 hours |
| P2 | Production degraded | 1 business day |
| P3 | Planned or cosmetic work | 5 business days |

The dispatcher agrees the priority with the customer when the call is logged. Escalate any P1 call that has no engineer assigned after 30 minutes to the Field Services duty manager.
