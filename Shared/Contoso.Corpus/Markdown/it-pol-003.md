# IT policy IT-POL-003: Using AI tools

Don't paste Contoso documents, drawings, customer data or personal data into public AI chatbots or translation sites. Their providers may store what you type.

Use the Contoso Knowledge Assistant for questions about Contoso's documents. It answers from documents you're allowed to read and shows its sources.
