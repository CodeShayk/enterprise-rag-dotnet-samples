# Field service report SR-2026-1213

Customer: Woodgrove Foods, packaging line 2. Engineer: Kwame Asante. Date: December 13, 2026.

Complaint: a whining noise from the HG-400 gearbox that rose with speed.

Findings: the oil level was below the sight glass and the oil had darkened; the last oil change was 14 months earlier. The first-stage gear teeth showed light pitting.

Work done: drained and flushed the gearbox, refilled it with 18 liters of ISO VG 220 oil and replaced a leaking output shaft seal. The noise dropped to normal. Recommended an oil analysis every six months.
