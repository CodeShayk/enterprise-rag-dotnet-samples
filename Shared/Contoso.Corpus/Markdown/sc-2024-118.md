# Supplier contract SC-2024-118: Fabrikam Seals

Contoso Industrial and Fabrikam Seals agree on the supply of mechanical seals for CP-200 pumps: PN-4471-B and, from September 2026, PN-4471-C.

The contract runs from July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2027 and renews for one year unless either party gives notice 90 days before it ends. Fabrikam Seals delivers within six weeks of an order and reports any quality incident that affects delivered parts within five business days.
