# Troubleshooting: excessive vibration

Vibration above 4.5 mm/s RMS on the bearing housing needs investigation; above 7.1 mm/s RMS, stop the pump.

Likely causes, in the order to check them:

- Misalignment between the pump and motor shafts after maintenance. Check the coupling alignment.
- Cavitation, which sounds like gravel rattling in the casing. It comes from low suction pressure: check that the suction valve is fully open and the suction strainer is clean.
- Worn bearings. Bearing noise rises before the vibration does.
- A loose baseplate or loose foot bolts.
- A damaged or unbalanced impeller.
