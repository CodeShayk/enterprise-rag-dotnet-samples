# Safety bulletin SB-0947: Hot work permits

Welding, grinding and cutting outside the workshops need a hot work permit from the shift supervisor. Remove or cover combustible material within 10 meters, keep a fire extinguisher at the work site and post a fire watch for 60 minutes after the work ends.

Hot work on a line that has carried oil or solvent also needs a gas test before the permit is signed.
