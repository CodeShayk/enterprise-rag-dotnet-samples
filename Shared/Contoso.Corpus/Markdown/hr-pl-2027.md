# HR guidance: parental leave changes for 2027

This guidance is restricted to HR and people managers until the change is announced on February 15, 2027. From March 1, 2027, paid parental leave increases from 16 to 20 weeks and can be taken in up to three blocks.

The six-month service requirement and full pay are unchanged. Employees whose leave starts before March 1, 2027 stay on the 2024 policy. Managers should send questions to their HR business partner.
