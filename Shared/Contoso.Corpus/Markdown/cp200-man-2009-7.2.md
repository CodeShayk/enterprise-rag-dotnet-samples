# 7.2 Service intervals

Inspect the mechanical seal and the bearings every 2,000 operating hours or six months, whichever comes first. Replace mechanical seal PN-4471-B every 8,000 operating hours.

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Inspect the mechanical seal and the bearings | 2,000 h or 6 months |
| Regrease the bearings (lithium-complex grease, 15 g per bearing) | 4,000 h |
| Check the coupling alignment | 12 months |
| Replace mechanical seal PN-4471-B | 8,000 h |
| Replace the bearings | 32,000 h |

Record every inspection in the maintenance log with the operating hours from the hour meter. Halve the intervals for pumps that handle abrasive liquids or run above 60 °C.
