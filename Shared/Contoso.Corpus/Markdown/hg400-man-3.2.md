# 3.2 Mounting

Mount the HG-400 on a flat, rigid base; the mounting surface must be flat to within 0.1 mm. Use the four M16 foot bolts of property class 8.8 and tighten them to 195 N·m in a cross pattern.

Put shims under the feet, never under the gearbox housing. After mounting, align the input and output couplings as their installation instructions describe, and check that the breather plug on top of the housing is fitted and open.
