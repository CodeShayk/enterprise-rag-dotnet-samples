# Error E-217: seal-water pressure low

Error E-217 means seal-water pressure low. The controller trips the pump when seal-water pressure stays below 1.5 bar for 10 seconds, to protect the mechanical seal. Check that seal-water valve V-114 is open and that its strainer is clean, then reset the alarm at the local panel.

Common causes, most likely first:

1. Seal-water valve V-114 left closed or partly closed after maintenance.
2. A blocked strainer upstream of V-114 (work instruction WI-FS-031 describes the cleaning).
3. A failed pressure switch PS-31. Compare its reading with the local gauge.

To clear E-217, restore the seal-water pressure to at least 2.0 bar, wait until the alarm light stops flashing, then press RESET on the local panel. If the alarm returns within an hour, don't restart the pump again; raise a work order for the seal-water system.
