# 5.4 Start-up

Before the first start, and after any work on the seal or the impeller, prime the pump and check the seal-water supply.

1. Open the suction valve fully. Never throttle the suction side.
2. Vent the casing at the vent plug until water flows without air.
3. Open the seal-water supply and confirm a pressure of 2.0 to 3.0 bar at the seal flush connection. The seal-water pressure must exceed the suction pressure by at least 1 bar.
4. Open the discharge valve about one quarter.
5. Jog the motor and confirm the direction of rotation against the arrow on the bearing housing.
6. Start the pump, then open the discharge valve slowly to the duty point within two minutes. Running against a closed discharge valve for longer overheats the liquid in the casing.

During the first hour, check that the bearing temperatures stay below 80 °C and listen for cavitation noise.
