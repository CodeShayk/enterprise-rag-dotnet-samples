# Error E-104: motor winding temperature high

Error E-104 means the motor's winding temperature exceeded 155 °C. The controller trips the pump and blocks a restart until the windings cool below 120 °C.

Check the motor's cooling fan and fan cover for blockages, and measure the supply voltage on all three phases; an unbalance of more than 2 percent overheats the windings. Frequent starts also cause E-104: the CP-200 motor allows no more than six starts per hour.

Reset E-104 at the local panel once the temperature has dropped. Never bypass the thermistor relay.
