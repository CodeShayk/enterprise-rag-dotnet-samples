# SOP-FS-014: Mechanical seal replacement on CP-200 pumps

Revision 3, effective September 14, 2026. This revision replaces PN-4471-B with PN-4471-C, following ECN-2291.

1. Apply lockout/tagout to the motor and isolate the seal-water and process lines, as safety bulletin SB-0932 requires. Verify zero energy.
2. Drain the casing and remove the coupling guard.
3. Remove the seal housing and the old seal. Inspect the shaft sleeve for scoring.
4. Fit mechanical seal PN-4471-C. Don't mix PN-4471-B and PN-4471-C parts.
5. Tighten the seal gland nuts evenly to 25 N·m.
6. Refit the coupling guard, remove the locks and tags, and start the pump as described in section 5.4 of the manual.
7. Record the seal's part number and the operating hours in the work order.
