# 4.1 Lubrication

The HG-400 is filled at the factory with 18 liters of ISO VG 220 mineral gear oil. Change the oil after the first 500 operating hours, then every 5,000 operating hours or every 12 months, whichever comes first. Synthetic PAO oil of the same viscosity extends the interval to 10,000 hours.

Check the oil level weekly at the sight glass with the gearbox stopped. In operation, the oil temperature must not exceed 90 °C.
