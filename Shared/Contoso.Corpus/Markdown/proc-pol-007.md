# Procurement policy PROC-POL-007: Purchase approvals

Every purchase order needs an approver other than the requester. Orders above 25,000 USD need a second approval from the plant controller, and orders above 250,000 USD need the CFO's approval.

Buy only from qualified suppliers. A new supplier is qualified after a quality audit and a credit check, and Procurement re-qualifies each supplier every three years.
