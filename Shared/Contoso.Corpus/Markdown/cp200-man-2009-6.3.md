# 6.3 Flange bolt torque

Table 6-2 gives the tightening torque for the CP-200 flange bolts. The values apply to bolts of property class 8.8, lightly oiled, with the spiral-wound gaskets supplied with the pump.

| Bolt size | Torque |
|---|---|
| M10 | 49 N·m |
| M12 | 85 N·m |
| M16 | 210 N·m |

Tighten the M12 flange bolts (property class 8.8, lightly oiled) to 85 N·m. Apply the torque in a cross pattern in three passes: 30 N·m, 60 N·m, then 85 N·m. Re-check the torque after the first 24 hours of operation.

Don't reuse spiral-wound gaskets. A gasket that has been compressed once won't seal at the specified torque.
