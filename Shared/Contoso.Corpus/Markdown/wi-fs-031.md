# WI-FS-031: Cleaning the seal-water strainer upstream of V-114

The seal-water line to pump P-201 has a Y-strainer with a 100-micron screen upstream of valve V-114. A blocked screen is the most common cause of error E-217 at the Riverside plant.

1. Stop P-201 and close the isolation valve upstream of the strainer. Leave V-114 open.
2. Put a bucket under the strainer and release the pressure through the blow-down valve.
3. Unscrew the strainer cap, pull out the screen and rinse it with clean water. Replace the screen if it is torn.
4. Refit the screen with a new cap gasket and open the isolation valve.
5. Check that the gauge shows a seal-water pressure of 2.0 to 3.0 bar.

Clean the screen every three months, and whenever E-217 trips.
