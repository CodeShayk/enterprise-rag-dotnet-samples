# Engineering change notice ECN-2291: CP-200 mechanical seal

Effective September 14, 2026, mechanical seal PN-4471-B is superseded by PN-4471-C on all CP-200 pumps. PN-4471-C has a silicon-carbide face rated for higher seal-water temperatures. Fit PN-4471-C at the next seal replacement and quarantine remaining PN-4471-B stock.

Reason for the change: three PN-4471-B failures in 2026 were traced to wear of the carbon face at seal-water temperatures above 60 °C.

Affected documents: CP-200 manual (2009) sections 7.2 and 8.1, and SOP-FS-014. The manual will be revised in 2027; until then, this notice takes precedence over it.

Approved by Nadia Rahman, reliability engineering.
