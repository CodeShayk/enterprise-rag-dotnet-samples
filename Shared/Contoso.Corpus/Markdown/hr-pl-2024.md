# Parental leave policy (2024)

Employees with at least six months of service receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave after the birth or adoption of a child. The leave must be taken in one continuous block within 12 months of the birth or adoption, at 100 percent of base salary.

Employees agree the start date with their manager at least eight weeks in advance.
