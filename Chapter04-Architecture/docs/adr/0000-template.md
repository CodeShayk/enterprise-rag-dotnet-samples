# ADR-NNNN: The decision, as a short imperative

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: names and roles

## Context

The forces at play: the requirement or quality attribute this serves, the constraints, the options considered and what each costs. Link the measurements, not opinions about them.

## Decision

What we decided, in one or two sentences, followed by the details someone needs to implement it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder and what we must now do because of this decision.

## Evidence

The measurements or tests that support the decision, with the command that reproduces them and the date they were run.

## Revisit when

The condition that reopens this decision: a measurement crossing a threshold, a new requirement or a change in what the market offers.
